## Notes (only what is certain)

- Girolamo Frachetta: Italian political writer in Rome in these years, the addressee named in the BnF catalogue.
- The League: the Catholic League, which held Paris in 1593 against Henri IV; the letters are in the League's polyphonic cipher (Tomokiyo).
- Henri IV abjured Protestantism at Saint-Denis on 25 July 1593, three days after this letter's date; a reference to a change of religion would be to that conversion as announced or expected.
- "Our legate": the papal legate in France at this date was Cardinal Filippo Sega.
