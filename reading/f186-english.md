## Doubtful stretches

The cipher of both pages was read with one or two passes only; the readable phrases above are those whose French is clear in the key's output, and everything between them is uncertain. "As king" (verso 21) rests on `pour ros` read as *pour roy*; "peace" (recto 26) and "pontificate" (recto 31) rest on partly garbled words. The clear text of the recto was read by eye and has its own doubtful words, marked [?] in the French file.

## Notes (only what is certain)

- Pietro Aldobrandini: cardinal-nephew of Pope Clement VIII, hence "His Holiness" and "Your Most Illustrious Lordship" side by side.
- The election of a king: the League's Estates-General sat in Paris in 1593 to choose a Catholic king against Henri IV; the house of Bourbon is Henri IV's house.
- Henri IV abjured Protestantism at Saint-Denis on 25 July 1593, three days after the letter's date; "wanting to make himself Catholic and go to mass" refers to that conversion before it happened.
- The truce between the League and the king was concluded after this date (end of July 1593); the letter cannot report it.
